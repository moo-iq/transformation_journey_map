# Transformation Journey Map
This project was created after using various canvas and A3 solutions in corporate environments. 
You will find several documents with specific topics and purposes. Those that are closely related are currently bundeled together.

## Sources
The documents are created with [https://draw.io] diagram app. You can use it for free in the browser or download the app.
Besides that I store some versions of the file as PDF exports for easy access.

## Overview of the canvas available

### Agile Transformation Journey
**Status:** draft
**Purpose:** We successfully managed various smaller and larger Lean-Agile transformations with or beyond known frameworks.
The agile transformation jourey is going to be a dedicated journey based on the general _Transformation Journey_ ackonwleding the special needs
of Lean-Agile engineering teams in various domains.
**References:** This journey is build on the ideas and achievements of [DACH30](https://dach30.net) and the [Grado network](https://grado.group)

### Organizational Canvas
**Status:** released
**Purpose:** This canvas is an analytical structure for an organization. 
It is based on the general concepts of the viable system model (VSM) and the cybernetic approach. 
In corporate environments I experienced the original sources, with their reference to the human body, to theoretical and not accepted.
The canvas design is a more "traditional" schema and therefore it is easier to use for discussions in corporate environments.
**References:** Just search for VSM or ask your preferred GPT. There are tons of references, I used various of them.

### Transformation Journey Map
**Status:** released
**Purpose:** This journey map was created to simplify transformations or change projects before they actually get kicked-off.
During the implementation of various transformation and change initiatives I realized that people tend to jump into it and try to make a clue out
of things such as Kotter or others. However they forget to ask themselves the underlying and actually initial questions.

As I strongly believe that the following elements belong closely together they are currently bundeled in one file, devided in 4 areas:
1. A journey map leading you towards the kick-off for your next transformation/change project/initiative
2. Blueprint for a complexity map in your projects, based on the Stacy Matrix evaulation of complexity
3. Experience Journey, to describe by which objectives your teams will be guided through the transformation and which key results are relevant to measure
4. Culture design framework, to understand, describe and envison the intended culture of your organization

**References:** 
* Transformation Journey and impulse is inspired by: Leading Transformations: Manage Change by Macquire University Sydney
* Stacy Matrix for projects within agile context @ [scrum.org](https://www.scrum.org/resources/blog/agile-right-fit)
* The experience journey is an own invention, enhanced by the concepts of Objectives and key results (OKRs)
* Culture design frame was inveted by Spencer Stuart and described in an [HBR article: the leaders guide to corporate culture](https://hbr.org/2018/01/the-leaders-guide-to-corporate-culture)
